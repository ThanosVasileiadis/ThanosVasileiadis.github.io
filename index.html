01

Research

Learning to make decisions
under uncertainty.

My research brings together reinforcement learning and optimal control, with a focus on distributional objectives, continuous state spaces, and interactions across large populations.

I.

Distributional reinforcement learning

Markov decision processes and policy gradient methods for learning reward distributions.

II.

Learning in continuous spaces

Kernel approximation methods and quantitative bounds for Q-learning.

III.

Mean field games & control

Exploration, common noise, and reinforcement learning in systems of interacting agents.

Related work includes portfolio optimisation under smooth ambiguity and index-option pricing with common noise. Applications also include deep hedging and autonomous control.
